Autonomous Ground Vehicle

Overview

This project was completed as part of my 2025 senior design capstone. The goal was to design and build a fully autonomous ground vehicle using the Robot Operating System 2 (ROS2).

My responsibilities included leading the development of the electronics and software stack, working in C, C++, ROS2, and Linux. I was also heavily involved in testing, debugging, and system validation.

Integration Components:

  • Nvidia Jetson Orin Nano
  • Teensy 4.0
  • Teknic clear path motors (4 48V motors)
  • Custom motor feedback boards
  • Zed2 Stero Camera
  • Oak-D Stereo Camera
  • Hokuyo UTM-30LX Lidar
  • 9-DOF imu
  • Varriying step down converters
