About Me

I'm a robotics engineer with a B.S. in Mechanical Engineering, robotics and mechatronics concentration, from Virginia Tech. I'm currently doing robotics software contract work for a startup, building on a background that mixes hardware and software pretty heavily.

I've worn a lot of hats along the way: Lead Undergraduate Researcher at Virginia Tech's Bio-Inspired Science and Technology Lab, a mechanical engineering co-op in heavy industry, leadership roles across school and engineering teams, and a lot of software engineering through both professional work and personal projects. I'm also entrepreneurial, I run Rilos 3D Solutions, an online shop where I sell 3D-printed parts I've designed and made myself.

When I'm not engineering, I'm usually building or tinkering with something. When I'm not doing that, I'm outdoors.
